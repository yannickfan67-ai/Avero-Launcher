PRINCIPLES

Not a port.
Not a WebView.

Native first

Kotlin + Compose, designed around Android interaction patterns.

Independent

A separate package and codebase instead of a thin client for the PC app.

Adaptive

Phone, tablet, foldable and desktop-window layouts are treated as first-class targets.

Open-ended

Git, repo hosting, terminal bridges and AI can arrive as clean modules later.
